## A little code. A little art. A lot of iteration.

Hey, I'm **Sanay** — I build **apps**, make **games**, and create **animations**. I also work on art, editing, and graphic design.

I like moving between the technical and creative sides of a project: building a gameplay system, shaping an interface, adjusting the timing of an animation, then trying it all again. The details matter to me — how something responds, what it communicates, and whether it feels good to use.

**My sweet spot:** programming that you can see, interact with, and play.

## A few of my projects

<a href="https://pigheadedpeak16.github.io/stormvault-courier-alpha/">
  <img src="assets/stormvault-controller.png" width="100%" alt="Stormvault Courier — one of my game development projects. A browser-game prototype built with TypeScript, Phaser, and Vite.">
</a>

Pilot a salvage ship through hostile sectors, collect resources, and decide when to push further or head back. **Stormvault Courier** brings together gameplay programming, progression balancing, interface design, and game feel.

- **Explore & survive:** solar-system routes, rising enemy pressure, and an enemy codex.
- **Build your ship:** class branches, upgrades for the current run, and permanent progression.
- **Play your way:** keyboard/mouse and small-screen touch controls.

*Playable alpha — more ship tiers, mechanics, and content are still in development.*

**[Play in your browser →](https://pigheadedpeak16.github.io/stormvault-courier-alpha/)** &nbsp; · &nbsp; [Source code](https://github.com/pigheadedpeak16/stormvault-courier-alpha) &nbsp; · &nbsp; [Controls](https://github.com/pigheadedpeak16/stormvault-courier-alpha#controls)

<br>

<a href="https://pigheadedpeak16.github.io/btc-lab-v3/">
  <img src="assets/btc-lab-workbench.png" width="100%" alt="BTC LAB — one of my software and data projects. A local strategy research workbench built with Python.">
</a>

A local research workbench for BTC five-minute prediction markets. **BTC LAB** focuses on inspectable experiments: where the data came from, which strategy version ran, and what the supplied order book allowed.

- **Configure:** seven strategy families with saved strategy revisions.
- **Replay:** historical observations with timing, fee, expiry, and liquidity checks.
- **Inspect:** Live Data, Strategies, Results, and Diagnostics views.

*Paper-only research software; it does not place real-money orders or establish profitable returns.*

**[Explore the project →](https://pigheadedpeak16.github.io/btc-lab-v3/)** &nbsp; · &nbsp; [Source code](https://github.com/pigheadedpeak16/btc-lab-v3) &nbsp; · &nbsp; [Research notes](https://github.com/pigheadedpeak16/btc-lab-v3/blob/main/docs/RESEARCH_NOTES.md)
